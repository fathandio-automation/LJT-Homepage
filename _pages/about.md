I am Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.ust.hk/), where I am a member of the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkunlp.github.io/) and am fortunate to be advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). Prior to my Ph.D., I received my B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning. I am particularly interested in building more capable and trustworthy large models.

Research Interests
======
* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** — improving the reasoning capabilities of large language models, including via reinforcement learning and synthesizing verifiable reasoning data.
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** — understanding and mitigating perception and hallucination problems in multimodal models.
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — probing the inner representations of LLMs to understand and improve their truthfulness.
